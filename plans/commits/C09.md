## Acceptance

## Required negative cases

- Missing, untrusted, expired, wrong-EKU and revoked client identities cannot ingest events and leave durable state unchanged.
- A stale or signature-forged revocation list, an unparseable anchor/listener certificate and 15 invalid configuration rows are rejected with typed errors.
- A replayed identity that later becomes revoked cannot ingest afterwards, and a scope mismatch fails before any storage side effect.
- RFC 5280 revocation identity is (issuer, serial): membership is matched to the CRL issuer, an intermediate-issued chain cannot authenticate while the single revocation authority is an anchor (fail-closed chain shape, documented restriction), and the accepted client-issuer set is the CRL issuer itself: with one locally delivered CRL, the guard refuses to authenticate anything whose issuer it cannot revoke, so every accepted collector stays revocable while unrelated serials are never falsely applied.
- No egress by default: the mTLS boundary connects loopback listeners only (no OCSP, directory or other network service).
